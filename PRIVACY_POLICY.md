# Privacy Policy for Sudoku Pop

Effective date: 2026-05-28

Sudoku Pop is a casual Sudoku puzzle game.

## Data Collection

Sudoku Pop does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data in version 1.0.

The app does not require an account and does not request access to personal information such as name, email address, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

## Local Game Data

Sudoku Pop may store gameplay progress, settings, and score records locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app functionality, such as continuing a saved game or showing best records.

This local data is not sent to the developer or to any external server by version 1.0.

## Advertising and Analytics

Sudoku Pop does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs in version 1.0.

If advertising, analytics, or payment features are added in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated before release.

## Children

Sudoku Pop is a general audience puzzle game. The app is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
